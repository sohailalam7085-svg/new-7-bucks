FAQs

Common
Questions

Do you show fixed prices for rentals?+
No — every project is different. Pricing depends on the equipment needed, duration, crew requirements, and location. Contact us with your brief and we'll send a custom quote within 24 hours.
Can I rent just a camera without a crew?+
Yes. Equipment-only rentals are available. We also offer operator-included options if you'd like someone to run the kit professionally.
How quickly can you set up a podcast recording space?+
For most podcast setups, we can be on-site and fully configured within a few hours of confirming the brief. Speak to us about your timeline and we'll work around it.
What types of shoots do you support?+
We work across branded content, documentaries, commercial shoots, corporate videos, podcast recordings, event coverage, and social media productions.
Do you provide crew alongside equipment?+
Yes. We can supply sound recordists, photographers, videographers, camera assistants, and production coordinators depending on your project scope.
What areas do you cover?+
We are based in Delhi and operate across the NCR region and beyond. For outstation shoots, travel arrangements are included in the custom quote.
